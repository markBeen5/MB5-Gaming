PRE-SNAP ANSWERS

COVERAGE BEATERS

Do not hunt for a perfect play. Recognize the shell and attack the space it gives you.

MAN / COVER 1

GUN BUNCH • MESH

Use crossers, drags and leverage. Throw as the receiver wins the break.

COVER 2

TRIPS • CORNER + SEAM

Read the safety. Corner if he stays inside; seam if the outside defender widens.

COVER 3

FLOOD / DAGGER

Stretch the outside third vertically, then hit the intermediate window underneath.

COVER 4

DRIVE / UNDERNEATH

Take the underneath space and make the deep defenders tackle. Stay patient.

HEAVY BLITZ

QUICK MESH / ZIG

Know the hot answer before the snap. Get the ball out instead of drifting backward.

MB5 GAME PLAN

START → READ → ADJUST

A simple flow to keep you from abandoning the scheme after one bad drive.

01START

Establish Gibbs

Make the defense respect the box and show how it wants to fit the run.

02IDENTIFY

Read the safeties

One high, two high, man leverage or pressure. Do not guess after the snap.

03ATTACK

Use the answer

Bunch for man, flood/dagger for zone, quick game for pressure.

04ADJUST

Take what stays open

If they remove the explosive, live underneath. If they crowd the box, punish it outside.
