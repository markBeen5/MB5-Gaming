01
PRIMARY FORMATION

GUN TRIPS TE

Use when you do not know what the defense is running.

PROGRESSION
  1. Amon-Ra / inside route
  2. TE intermediate route
  3. Gibbs underneath
  4. Deep receiver if the safety bites
VS COVER 3

Look seam or corner first. If the deep defender stays over it, hit the intermediate route. If the linebacker drops underneath, hit Gibbs.

GOFF THROW: bullet or touch with a small lead.
02
MAN BEATER

GUN BUNCH

Attack aggressive man coverage.

PROGRESSION

Point receiver → crossing route → flat

VS COVER 1 MAN

Look for the receiver crossing underneath the linebackers. Lead slightly upfield with the left stick and throw as he crosses the defender—do not wait until he is five yards open.

03
COVER 2 KILLER

GUN TRIPS

When you see two safeties high, think corner plus seam.

READ THE SAFETY

If the safety stays inside, throw the corner. If the outside corner drops deep, hit the seam. If both are covered, check down to Gibbs underneath.

04
STOP THE BLITZ

SINGLEBACK PLAY ACTION

Run the ball a few times first, then call play action.

PRIMARY READ

TE or Amon-Ra on the intermediate route.

WHEN LINEBACKERS BITE

Throw immediately behind them. Do not wait for five yards of separation; keep Goff throwing from a stable pocket.

05
2-MINUTE OFFENSE

GUN EMPTY

Use when you are behind or need a conversion.

PROGRESSION

Amon-Ra → TE → middle → RB / short route

VS MAN

Crossers, slants and drags

VS COVER 3

Seam, dig and curl

VS COVER 2

Middle and corner

RED ZONE PACKAGE

GUN BUNCH X NASTYVS ZONE

Z SPOT GOALLINE

Read: flat → spot → corner. Throw the flat if the outside defender sinks; hit the spot when the hook defender widens.

GUN BUNCH X NASTYVS MAN / PRESSURE

Z MESH GOALLINE

Read: first crosser → second crosser → checkdown. Let the mesh create traffic and get the ball out before the rush arrives.

GUN BUNCH X NASTYQUICK ANSWER

Y FLAT GOALLINE

Read: leverage at the snap. Take the fast flat when the defender is inside; move to the goal-line route if the flat is covered.

GUN DOUBLES HIPVS MAN

BENCH HB ANGLE

Read: Gibbs on the angle against a linebacker, then the bench route outside. Throw as Gibbs makes his cut—do not wait.

GUN TRIO Y-FLEXBOX COUNT

RPO ALERT BUBBLE

Read: light box, hand it to Gibbs. Extra defender inside, throw the bubble when the offense has numbers outside.

INSIDE THE 5

GOAL LINE PACKAGE

GOAL LINE NORMALPRIMARY RUN

HB POWER G

Call it: when the interior box is balanced. Follow the pulling guard with Gibbs and press the hole before making one cut.

GOAL LINE NORMALSHORT YARDAGE

HB DIVE

Call it: when you need one or two yards and the A-gap is not overloaded. Get downhill immediately.

GOAL LINE NORMALINCHES ONLY

QB SNEAK

Call it: on the 1-yard line or fourth-and-inches when neither A-gap is stacked. Do not use it into a pinched interior.

GOAL LINE NORMALRUN COUNTER

PA SPOT

Read: flat → spot. Use it after showing power; if the edge defender attacks the run, throw quickly behind him.

GOAL LINE NORMALEDGE MOVEMENT

PA WAGGLE

Read: crossing route → flat. Use the moving pocket when the defense crashes hard inside, but throw it away if the edge stays home.

GOAL LINE NORMALPINCHED MIDDLE

STRONG TOSS

Call it: when the defensive line pinches and the edge is soft. Get outside first, then cut north behind the lead blocker.

MB5 RULE: Run until the defense adds a box defender. Then answer with the matching quick pass or RPO—no forced throws across the middle.
