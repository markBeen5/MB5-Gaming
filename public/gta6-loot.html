Evidence standard

MB5 has not independently reproduced the newly reported post-launch locations or rewards; consult linked sources before relying on routes. Trailer sightings and community claims must not be presented as proven playable discoveries.

To verify an entry: retain a direct source link and timestamp, identify the game build, capture the map location and interaction steps, document the reward, and reproduce it independently. Unknown details stay explicitly unknown.
