CIVILIAN PERKS • CURRENT RULES

🃏 PERK CARDS & DECKS

Completing matches earns Perk Points. Spend them to roll randomized Perk Cards, with larger spends improving your chance at higher-rarity cards. Owned cards can also be temporarily upgraded for a set number of matches.

1 • EARN

Finish matches to earn Perk Points.

2 • ROLL

Spend Perk Points on randomized cards; higher spends can improve rarity odds.

3 • BUILD

Equip collected cards in Civilian Perk Decks, then receive randomized perk choices during matches.

PATCH 1.0.1 PERK CHANGES

  • Maximum 2 copies of the same perk per deck. Existing decks over the limit automatically have extra copies unequipped.
  • The amount of XP required to reach the initial in-match perk rolls was increased.
  • A bug that could leave the in-game perk rolling menu stuck was fixed.
  • Delay on perk-related interactions in the Graveyard was reduced.
Early Access bonus: qualifying players receive 5,000 free Perk Points. IllFonic notes that the points may take extra time to appear.
XP • CHALLENGES • PRESTIGE

📈 PROGRESSION SYSTEM

Match and Story Mode XP feeds four distinct progression categories. Increasing them unlocks playable Civilians, Michael abilities, Civilian starting items, cosmetics, executions, challenge tiers and other rewards.

PROFILE LEVEL

Overall progression using XP earned across roles and modes.

KILLER LEVEL

Progress earned while playing Michael Myers.

CIVILIAN LEVEL

Separate progression tied to each playable Civilian.

WEAPON LEVEL

Progress earned by killing victims with Michael's specific equipable weapons.

PRESTIGE

Each of the four progression categories can be prestiged after reaching its maximum level, giving dedicated players additional rewards and a way to show long-term progression.

THREE CHALLENGE TYPES

PASSIVE

Automatically tracked across multiple Killer and Civilian categories.

PROGRESSIVE

Sequential objectives tied to specific Civilians, Michael skins and weapon types.

SINGLEPLAYER

Optional objectives across all six Story Mode chapters with alternate outfits and unique cosmetics.

COMPLETION REWARD

Completing every singleplayer challenge unlocks a legendary look for committed Michael players.

IllFonic has confirmed hundreds of challenges, but it has not published a complete official XP table, every level cap, or every reward threshold.
LIVE BUILD HISTORY

🛠 PATCHES & HOTFIXES

PATCH 1.0.1 — SEPTEMBER 8, 2026

CROSS-PLAY

Cross-play party members now follow the party leader into matchmade lobbies instead of being kicked to the main menu or dropped from the party.

ATHLETICISM 6–10

Max Stamina and Stamina regeneration were slightly reduced for higher-Athleticism Civilians to keep chase tension balanced.

ESCAPES

Civilians now discover Escape objectives from farther away. IllFonic is also investigating additional Civilian onboarding improvements.

MATCH BALANCE

End-of-match scoring adjusted; low-speed vehicle force reduced; Broken Down Car max health increased; public-lobby pre-match countdown increased when not full.

MICHAEL

Repeated blind diminishing returns increased, Detection Pulse is better at finding residents in hiding spots, and Stalk Markers last longer after line of sight breaks.

STALK / GRABS

Stalk level 1–2 targets have an easier time struggling free when grabbed. Multiple Michael visual/ability bugs were also fixed.

STORY + NPCS

NPC struggle timing now scales correctly with Story difficulty, Chapter 5 achievement issues were fixed, plus AI/pathing and crash fixes.

WORLD FIXES

Variable weather in Public Matches was fixed, along with disappearing prompts, escape-body cleanup and several interaction issues.

EARLY ACCESS HOTFIX 1 — SEPTEMBER 5, 2026

  • Numerous crash fixes.
  • Several challenge-tracking fixes.
  • Offline Play no longer affected by the login queue system.
  • Fixed Michael's missing get-up prompt after being downed.
  • Fixed incorrect Xbox Series S Performance Mode rendering on certain displays.
