HEROES OF HADDONFIELD • BUILD SYSTEM

🏃 CIVILIAN STATS & BUILDS

Each Civilian has unique stats and a signature trait. Loadouts support cosmetic customization, unlockable starting items and Civilian Perk Decks.

🏃 Athleticism

Movement speed and efficiency on foot or bicycle.

🗣️ Personality

Convincing residents, alerting authorities, follower control and resistance to Fear/Stalk.

🧰 Resourcefulness

Repairs, environmental awareness and effectiveness with vehicles and firearms.

💪 Capability

Vigor and effectiveness with melee weapons and thrown household objects.

THE CIVILIAN GAME PLAN

Warn and rescue residents → arm yourself → coordinate with teammates → alert law enforcement → survive Michael's pursuit.

CUSTOM LOADOUT SYSTEM

🃏 CIVILIAN PERK DECKS

Matches award Perk Points. Those points feed a randomized Perk Card system used to build customized Civilian decks.

1 • EARN

Complete matches and collect Perk Points.

2 • ROLL

Spend points for randomized Perk Cards. More points can improve higher-rarity odds.

3 • BUILD

Combine collected cards into Perk Decks for your Civilian loadouts.

Owned cards can also be temporarily upgraded for a set number of matches. Complete card names and exact numerical bonuses were not public in the Sept. 2 breakdown, so MB5 does not invent them.
