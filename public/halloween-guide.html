CIVILIAN PERKS • CURRENT RULES

🃏 PERK CARDS & DECKS

Completing matches earns Perk Points. Spend them to roll randomized Perk Cards, with larger spends improving your chance at higher-rarity cards. Owned cards can also be temporarily upgraded for a set number of matches.

1 • EARN

Finish matches to earn Perk Points.

2 • ROLL

Spend Perk Points on randomized cards; higher spends can improve rarity odds.

3 • BUILD

Equip collected cards in Civilian Perk Decks, then receive randomized perk choices during matches.

PATCH 1.0.1 PERK CHANGES

  • Maximum 2 copies of the same perk per deck.
  • XP required for the initial in-match perk rolls increased.
  • Stuck in-game perk rolling menu fixed.
  • Perk-related interaction delay in Graveyard reduced.
XP • CHALLENGES • PRESTIGE

📈 PROGRESSION SYSTEM

Match and Story Mode XP feeds four progression categories and unlocks Civilians, abilities, starting items, cosmetics, executions and challenge tiers.

PROFILE LEVEL

Overall XP across roles and modes.

KILLER LEVEL

Progress earned as Michael Myers.

CIVILIAN LEVEL

Separate progression for each Civilian.

WEAPON LEVEL

Progress from kills with specific Michael weapons.

CHALLENGES & PRESTIGE

Passive, Progressive and Singleplayer Challenges provide additional unlocks. Each progression category can prestige at max level.

🛠 PATCHES & QUALITY-OF-LIFE

PATCH 1.1.0 — OCTOBER 6, 2026

PERFORMANCE

Improved CPU/GPU performance across maps, DLSS/FSR/XeSS performance, DLSS-G support and Xbox Series S stability.

MATCH PACING

Escape discovery now scales with match time, escape-item spawns were reduced/rebalanced, and Storm Cellar/Gate escape capacity was lowered.

MICHAEL

Bloodthirst now rewards Stalking and Executions more heavily, early-tier Shape Jump was slowed, Shape Dash was sped up and late-game stalking was strengthened.

CIVILIANS

Sprint stamina use increased, Car Repair no longer requires holding the interaction button, and Pocket Knife struggle difficulty now scales with Stalk/Bloodthirst.

UI + QOL

Added self-location on the map, Spectator Minigame toggle, clearer Perk Point totals, updated icons and improved notifications/HUD behavior.

NEW CONTENT

One new Context Execution was added alongside map lighting/set-dressing polish and Story Mode environment/cinematic improvements.

PATCH 1.0.4 — SEPTEMBER 26, 2026

High-priority stability work, Xbox Series X|S improvements and corrections to the Grab/Struggle changes introduced in 1.0.3. IllFonic also granted 10,000 Perk Points to current players.
