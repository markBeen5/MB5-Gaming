🔪 MICHAEL MYERS LOADOUT

Michael begins with two core traits and can equip three additional abilities. Progression unlocks more abilities, weapons, executions, skins and tints.

Killer Sense

A starting trait designed to help Michael locate and stalk Haddonfield's residents.

Shape Jump

Michael's signature darkness-based movement. He can disappear into darkness and reappear in another dark location outside a Civilian's line of sight. He cannot enter or exit Shape Jump in direct light or while a Civilian is watching him.

Reality Tear

One of the selectable Michael abilities confirmed in the September 2 progression reveal. Exact launch values and cooldown details were not publicly specified in that reveal.

Blackout

A confirmed selectable ability. The September 2 reveal names it as part of Michael's customizable ability pool; exact numerical effects were not specified.

Detection Pulse

A confirmed selectable Michael ability intended for his hunting toolkit. Exact range/cooldown numbers were not published in the September 2 progression details.

Important: Michael grows stronger as a match progresses, so surviving longer also means dealing with an increasingly dangerous Shape.

🏃 CIVILIAN STATS & BUILDS

Each Civilian has unique stats and a signature trait. Loadouts support cosmetic customization, unlockable starting items and Civilian Perk Decks.

Athleticism

Controls how quickly and efficiently a Civilian travels on foot or by bicycle.

Personality

Affects convincing residents, alerting authorities, commanding extra followers, and resistance to Fear and Stalk.

Resourcefulness

Influences repairs, environmental awareness, and effectiveness with vehicles and firearms.

Capability

Represents vigor and effectiveness with melee weapons and throwable household objects.

CIVILIAN OBJECTIVE

Heroes work together to warn and rescue Haddonfield residents, arm themselves with weapons or household items, alert law enforcement and survive Michael's pursuit.

🃏 CIVILIAN PERK DECKS

Completing matches awards Perk Points. Those points can be spent on randomized rolls for Perk Cards that provide gameplay boosts.

  • Spend more Perk Points on a roll to improve the chance of a higher-rarity card.
  • Perk Points can temporarily upgrade cards you already own for a set number of matches.
  • Collected cards can be assembled into Perk Decks and equipped to Civilian loadouts.
  • The randomized card system is designed to vary builds and matches.

Specific complete perk-card names and exact numerical bonuses were not included in the September 2 public progression breakdown, so this page does not invent them.

📈 PROGRESSION & CHALLENGES

XP feeds four progression categories:

  • Profile Level — all XP regardless of role.
  • Killer Level — earned while playing Michael.
  • Civilian Level — progression tied to each playable Civilian.
  • Weapon Level — earned by killing with specific Michael weapons.

Leveling unlocks Civilians, Michael abilities, Civilian starting items, cosmetics, executions and higher challenge tiers. Maxed categories can be prestiged for additional rewards.

HUNDREDS OF CHALLENGES

Passive challenges track automatically across Killer/Civilian play. Progressive challenges are tied to particular Civilians, Michael skins and weapons. Six single-player chapters also contain optional challenges with cosmetics and a legendary reward for completing every story challenge.

🗺️ ALL 4 LAUNCH MAPS

EAST HADDONFIELD

Rural and industrial outskirts centered on the Midwest Feed Company grain mill, silos, farmland, highways and train tracks. Includes Phelps Garage and the neon-lit Rabbit in Red Lounge. Its openness, uneven fences and tangled structures create unusual sight lines and stealth routes.

HADDONFIELD HEIGHTS

A quiet residential neighborhood with modest homes, the Strode house, the abandoned Myers house on Lampkin Lane and a large water tower. A classic suburban Haddonfield setting.

ORANGE GROVE ESTATES

An upscale suburb with large multi-story homes, garages, balconies, fences, hedges and a neighborhood park. Includes the iconic Doyle and Wallace houses. Bikes and repaired vehicles can play into traversal.

HADDONFIELD TOWN CENTER

Downtown Haddonfield featuring Nichols Hardware, A-Side Music Store, Hill Garden Center, Patty's Pub, the post office, Margie's Auto Repair and the cemetery.

👥 PLAYABLE CIVILIANS

The Standard Edition launches with 10 Civilians: Jennifer, Tanya, Rachel, Eric, Marcus, Thomas, Bob Simms, Lynda van der Klok, Annie Brackett and Laurie Strode. The Digital Deluxe Edition adds Richard and Alexis as two exclusive playable Civilians.

Legacy characters Bob, Lynda, Annie and Laurie also appear in the single-player story. Story challenges can unlock alternate costumes and cosmetic items.
