🔪 MICHAEL MYERS LOADOUT

Michael begins with two core traits and can equip three additional abilities. Progression unlocks more abilities, weapons, executions, skins and tints.

Killer Sense

A starting trait designed to help Michael locate and stalk Haddonfield's residents.

Shape Jump

Michael's signature darkness-based movement. He can disappear into darkness and reappear in another dark location outside a Civilian's line of sight. He cannot enter or exit Shape Jump in direct light or while a Civilian is watching him.

Reality Tear

A confirmed selectable Michael ability. Exact launch values and cooldown details were not publicly specified in the September 2 reveal.

Blackout

A confirmed selectable ability in Michael's customizable ability pool; exact numerical effects were not specified.

Detection Pulse

A confirmed selectable hunting ability. Exact range and cooldown numbers were not published in the September 2 details.

Match pressure: Michael grows stronger as a match progresses.

🏃 CIVILIAN STATS & BUILDS

Each Civilian has unique stats and a signature trait. Loadouts support cosmetic customization, unlockable starting items and Civilian Perk Decks.

Athleticism

Controls how quickly and efficiently a Civilian travels on foot or by bicycle.

Personality

Affects convincing residents, alerting authorities, commanding extra followers, and resistance to Fear and Stalk.

Resourcefulness

Influences repairs, environmental awareness, and effectiveness with vehicles and firearms.

Capability

Represents vigor and effectiveness with melee weapons and throwable household objects.

CIVILIAN OBJECTIVE

Heroes work together to warn and rescue residents, arm themselves, alert law enforcement and survive Michael's pursuit.

🃏 CIVILIAN PERK DECKS

Completing matches awards Perk Points, which can be spent on randomized rolls for Perk Cards that provide gameplay boosts.

  • Spend more points to improve the chance of higher-rarity cards.
  • Temporarily upgrade owned cards for a set number of matches.
  • Assemble collected cards into Perk Decks for Civilian loadouts.

Complete perk-card names and exact numerical bonuses were not included in the September 2 public progression breakdown, so MB5 does not guess them.

📈 PROGRESSION & CHALLENGES

XP feeds Profile, Killer, Civilian and Weapon progression. Leveling unlocks Civilians, Michael abilities, starting items, cosmetics, executions and higher challenge tiers. Maxed categories can be prestiged.

HUNDREDS OF CHALLENGES

Passive and progressive challenges span multiplayer, while six single-player chapters contain optional challenges and cosmetic rewards.

🗺️ ALL 4 LAUNCH MAPS

EAST HADDONFIELD

Rural/industrial outskirts centered on a huge grain mill with silos, farmland, highways and train tracks, plus Phelps Garage and the Rabbit in Red Lounge.

HADDONFIELD HEIGHTS

Classic residential Haddonfield with the Strode house, abandoned Myers house on Lampkin Lane and the neighborhood water tower.

ORANGE GROVE ESTATES

Upscale suburb with large homes, garages, balconies and a park, including the iconic Doyle and Wallace residences.

HADDONFIELD TOWN CENTER

Downtown featuring Nichols Hardware, A-Side Music Store, Hill Garden Center, Patty's Pub and the cemetery.

👥 PLAYABLE CIVILIANS

The Standard Edition launches with 10 Civilians: Jennifer, Tanya, Rachel, Eric, Marcus, Thomas, Bob Simms, Lynda van der Klok, Annie Brackett and Laurie Strode. Digital Deluxe adds Richard and Alexis.

Legacy characters Bob, Lynda, Annie and Laurie also appear in the single-player story, where challenges can unlock alternate costumes and cosmetics.
