A NEW STORY IN HELLRAISER'S CANON

🩸 STORY

You play as Aidan, who must use the mysterious Genesis Configuration puzzle box to help his girlfriend Sunny escape the otherworldly hellscape of the Labyrinth. In doing so, Aidan becomes entangled with Pinhead's ritual and a cult devoted to the Cenobites.

Solve the configuration. Survive what answers.

CONFIRMED CHARACTERS & FORCES

👁 WHO'S IN REVIVAL?

AIDAN

The playable protagonist. He unlocks and uses the Genesis Configuration while trying to save Sunny.

SUNNY

Aidan's girlfriend, trapped in the Labyrinth and central to his journey into Hellraiser's nightmare.

PINHEAD

The iconic Cenobite returns with Doug Bradley voicing the role for the first time in nearly two decades.

THE CENOBITES

Infernal priests tied to the Labyrinth and the horrors Aidan unleashes.

CULTISTS

A twisted cult devoted to Pinhead and the Cenobites stands between Aidan and survival.

HELL'S CREATURES

The official game description also promises wretches, deviants and infernal monstrosities across the campaign.

FIRST-PERSON ACTION HORROR SURVIVAL

⚔️ GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

GENESIS CONFIGURATION

Use the puzzle box's hellish abilities in combat and survival situations.

MELEE + RANGED COMBAT

Combine supernatural powers with an arsenal of earthly melee and ranged weapons.

SURVIVAL HORROR

A story-driven campaign built around exploration, combat, horror pressure and resource survival.

OFFICIAL FAQ NOTES

  • No internet connection is required to play.
  • Expected playthrough length is roughly 8–12 hours depending on playstyle.
  • PC storage requirement is 80 GB, with an SSD recommended.
  • Platforms: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and Steam.
